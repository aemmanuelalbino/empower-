About Us

Who We Are

EGI is a system-building and capacity-development institution, not a short-term implementing agency. Built on decades of practical experience, professional continuity and trusted partnerships across Africa and Europe, we work with families, teachers, youth, community leaders, institutions and local partners.

Vision & Core Values

Vision: A world where children and young people develop into peaceful, creative individuals reaching their full potential.

  • Participatory democracy · Integrity · Collaboration
  • Innovation · Cooperation · Sustainability

Our History

40+ years in child development and education, with senior resource persons engaged since EGI's earliest beginnings — exceptional professional continuity as a core institutional asset.

Governance

General Assembly · International Board · Executive Board/Director · National Organisation Boards.

Our Programmes

Early Childhood Development

Comprehensive ECD from conception to eight years, including children with special needs, parental involvement and teacher training.

Community, Family & Youth

Empowering community leaders, youth and families to become agents of change.

Training Systems

Certificate, diploma and degree-level pathways for teachers, mentors and ECD trainers, with universities and national institutions.

WASH

Water, sanitation and hygiene integrated into model programmes, communities and all training.

Environment & Green Energy

Conservation, STEM learning, renewable energy and climate resilience.

Cross-Cutting Programmes

Peace promotion, agriculture and nutrition, sport and creativity, vocational training, counselling and life skills.

Our Approach

Model-Programme Logic

EGI develops, validates and refines high-quality model programmes first, then adapts and replicates them through structured partnerships, National Organisations and a shared digital learning platform.

Pillar 1 — Learning Materials

Books, curricula and training materials embedded within national systems as a shared quality standard.

Pillar 2 — Community Leaders

A multiplier model where trained leaders train teachers, mentor practitioners and support curricula.

Theory of Change

Resources→ Activities→ Outputs→ Outcomes→ Impact

Our Impact

Expected Impact

  • More children ready for school and life
  • Families gain knowledge, confidence and support
  • Teachers and leaders receive training and mentoring
  • Communities improve health, hygiene and resilience
  • Children protected from exclusion and violence
